🚀 **Google Photos Memory Retrieval – An AI-Powered Product Management Case Study**

I'm excited to share another project from my Product Management journey, where I explored an interesting problem around photo discovery and memory retrieval.

### 🔍 The Problem

Have you ever tried finding an old photograph but couldn't remember the exact date, filename or keywords?

You might remember:
- The people who were there.
- The trip or event.
- The place you visited.
- A special moment from years ago.

But translating these memories into searchable keywords isn't always easy.

**The challenge:** People remember the story behind a photograph, while search systems often expect descriptions of what's actually in the image.

### 💡 My Product Idea: AI-Powered Memory Retrieval

I explored how an AI-powered memory retrieval experience could help users find photographs using incomplete or vague memories.

The proposed solution focuses on five stages:

1. **Express:** Let users describe memories naturally without requiring exact dates or keywords.
2. **Interpret:** Use AI to extract clues such as people, places, events and approximate timelines.
3. **Retrieve:** Search and surface relevant photo candidates.
4. **Evaluate:** Show why each photograph matches the user's description.
5. **Refine:** Allow users to reject results, add clues and improve the search.

### 📊 User Research & Key Findings

As part of the project, I explored:
- 10 user interviews.
- 1,472 public reviews related to photo discovery.
- 9 real survey cases.
- 24-case discovery-engine dataset (including 15 synthetic demo rows).

Some interesting findings:

- 7 out of 9 real survey cases ended with a partial retrieval.
- 12 out of 24 recorded failures happened during memory expression and interpretation, before photo ranking.
- People, places and events were among the most commonly remembered clues.

These findings helped me identify the biggest opportunity: making it easier for users to express and refine vague memories.

### 🛠️ What I Built

I developed two interactive prototypes:

**1. Visual Oracle Vault – Memory Discovery Experience**

An AI-powered concept exploring how users can retrieve photographs through incomplete memories and contextual clues.

**2. Recall Finder Engine – Discovery & Research Engine**

A discovery tool designed to analyse user feedback, identify retrieval problems and understand where users struggle during photo searches.

### 🎯 Product Metrics

I defined the primary success metric as:

**Vague-Memory Retrieval Success Rate**

The percentage of retrieval tasks that end with users confirming the correct photograph.

I also explored supporting metrics:
- Time to first relevant photo.
- Time to successful retrieval.
- First-query relevance.
- Result rejection rate.
- Refinement effectiveness.
- Search abandonment rate.

### ⚠️ Product Risks I Considered

- Over-confident AI predictions.
- Privacy of personal photographs.
- Search scalability and indexing gaps.
- User fatigue from repeated follow-up questions.

The proposed experience makes AI-inferred clues visible and allows users to correct them.
